How I think

Start with the work.
Not the stack.

I start with the people, the information and the decisions involved. Then I choose what the system actually needs, and build the pieces to work together.

To me the tech doesn’t matter, solving the problem does.

Understand the work.

Find where things get stuck, what people need and which decisions matter.

Connect the right pieces.

Use models, data and software where they help. Keep control where it belongs.

Build, test, refine.
