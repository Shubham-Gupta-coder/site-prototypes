# v9.5 editorial decisions

This is an experience refinement, not a new biography or film script. The approved opening, film captions, personal belief, approach, five capability explanations and Verslay experience are retained.

| Area | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Hero navigation | Same helpful anchors; visible only in the opening/loading | Give way to the film, rather than competing with it or covering the normal ending. |
| Progress | A named progressbar, with no printed percentage or new slogan | Show the real position quietly; avoid a fake completion display or noisy announcement. |
| Approach | Retain “Start with the work. Not the stack.” and its concrete paragraph | Strong copy needs more intentional composition, not more slogans. |
| Belief | Retain “To me the tech doesn’t matter, solving the problem does.” | Preserve the supplied voice; use a crafted inset to give it prominence. |
| Method | Keep Understand the work / Connect the right pieces / Build, test, refine and explanations | Connect their existing mechanisms visually, rather than inventing a proprietary methodology. |
| Capabilities | Keep all five titles and descriptions; add Context → action, Source → context, System → interface, Build → operation, Tools → workflow | Short relations explain the selectable drawing. These are not client results or new credentials. |
| Atlas instruction | “Select a capability to trace the connection.” | Describe a real native interaction. Essential explanations remain visible without interaction. |
| Experience | Verslay / Technical founder / “Built autonomous AI systems for businesses across India.” / “I built Verslay’s technology infrastructure. Today, a tech team of five or fewer runs the company’s SaaS platform.” | The user supplied the infrastructure ownership and current team-size fact in this revision. It replaces the former platform-only proof once; no other headcount, outcome or employer is invented. |
| Contact | Keep natural invitation and WhatsApp; add “Find me on LinkedIn” | The supplied profile is an equally clear secondary destination, without CTA repetition elsewhere. |
| Ending return | Keep Back to the film | A real way back after the hero navbar intentionally leaves. |

No project parade, private CV material, extra employers, metrics, stack proficiency, fabricated outcomes or new story scenes. No automatic sending or profile research.

Responsive refinement: core film and personal prose remain unchanged. Nearby “Selected connection” feedback makes a capability choice understandable without looking back to a diagram on a narrow display. The feedback describes the actual native radio state, not a proficiency score or client outcome.
